2. Purpose and target audience
Orusu Maho (おるすマホ) is a digital wellbeing app for people in Japan who want to spend less time on social media, short videos, and games. The user "leaves the phone at home" for a chosen time, and while the phone is put down, a pixel-art companion travels across Japan: 1 minute of focus moves the companion 1 km. Reaching a landmark delivers a postcard and a local souvenir that decorate the companion's room. During a trip, the apps the user selected are paused with Apple's Screen Time API. The app has no ads.

3. How to use the main features
No login or sample files are required.
- Launch the app and follow the welcome screens. Tap "許可する" to allow Screen Time access (Family Controls, individual authorization), then tap "止めるアプリを選ぶ" to choose apps to pause.
- Tap "練習の旅に出る" to try a 10-second practice trip. Opening a paused app during the trip shows the shield screen with a letter from the companion. The setup can be skipped with "あとで設定する" or "練習せずにはじめる".
- Room tab: choose a length (15 minutes to 2 hours, or 5 to 180 minutes with the slider) and tap "旅に出る". The chosen apps are paused until the trip ends. A trip can be ended early with "旅を切り上げる"; the distance walked so far is kept.
- Settings > 外しにくさ (how hard to unlock): "やさしい" opens a paused app for 1 minute, "ふつう" opens it after waiting 10 seconds, and "きびしい" keeps it paused until the trip ends.
- Map tab: the current journey and the next landmark. Postcards tab: collected postcards. "道のりを変える" switches between journeys.
- The Room tab also shows today's screen time (DeviceActivityReport). This is drawn by iOS on the device; the app cannot read or send the usage data.

4. External services
- Apple Screen Time API (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, DeviceActivity): choosing apps, pausing them during a trip, ending the pause, and showing today's usage. All data stays on the device.
- Google Forms: the contact form opened from Settings (opens in the browser; only the device model, iOS version and app version are pre-filled).
- App Store: the "write a review" link in Settings.
The app does not make any network requests and we do not operate any server. No AI services, analytics, advertising, data providers, or third-party authentication are used.

5. Regional differences
The app is distributed only in Japan, and its features and content are the same for all users.
